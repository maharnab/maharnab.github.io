## The problem

A quadrant photodiode (QPD) reports where a laser spot sits on its four segments. To keep a beam aligned, the
QPD's error signals must be mapped to actuator corrections. That mapping is a control (or interaction) matrix,
which has to be measured and inverted reliably.

## What I did

Developed the control matrix relating QPD position signals to beam-steering actuators, in MATLAB, as part of the
IPR Summer Student Programme 2015.
