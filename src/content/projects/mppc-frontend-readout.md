## The problem

SiPMs produce fast, small current pulses on top of a significant dark-count rate. A calorimeter readout needs a
front end (bias supply, preamplifier and shaping) that preserves the signal while keeping noise and power low.

## What I did

- Designed a front-end circuit for Hamamatsu MPPCs and simulated its response.
- Built and tested it at the IIT Guwahati HEP lab.
- Presented it as "Frontend Readout Prototype for FGT-ECAL of DUNE" at the IIT Guwahati Research Conclave in
  2016, with follow-up work in my PhD thesis (chapter 3).
